                 Neote Keywords (guide)
              

              
Translated automatically in JS logic lines (ignored inside strings and $ / < lines):

              

                

                  showcase(...)
                  console.log(...)
                

                

                  st x = 10
                  let x = 10
                

                

                  notify("Hi")
                  alert("Hi")
                

                

                  qstn("Name?")
                  prompt("Name?")
                

              

            


            
            

              

                 $ DOM Loaders & /attr
              

              

                
$hd("text") → <header>

                
$btn("text") → <button>

                
$p("text") → <p>

                
$input("ph") → <input>

                
$sctn("txt") → <section>

                
$div("text") → <div>

                
$img("src") → <img>

                
$loader("url") → <iframe>

                
$ft("text") → <footer>

                
$href("u","t") → <a>

              

              

                Inline Attributes:

                $btn("Click") /class="btn" /id="go"
